Module 03 — Sustained Attention
AX-CPT: Sequence Detection
Letters flash one at a time. You must press SPACE (or tap below) only when you see X immediately after A. Do not press for X after any other letter — that's the trick. And do not press during A itself. Only A→X triggers a response. Stay focused: 40 letters total.
Press: …A… X → press SPACE on the X  |  Don't press: …B… X → ignore the X  |  …A… K → ignore the K

Module 04 — Fluid Reasoning
Pattern Completion
Each puzzle shows a 3×3 grid with the last piece missing. Patterns may follow number sequences, directional rules, letter progressions, or visual counts. Look for what changes across rows and columns simultaneously. Click the option that completes the pattern.
Tip: Check if numbers multiply or add — arrows rotate — letters advance — or counts increase by a rule. Some puzzles use two rules at once.
