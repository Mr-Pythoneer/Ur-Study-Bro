Bring your own API key — Gemini, Claude, GPT-4, or Groq. Set it once and every AI feature in the app uses it.

AI Tutor

A full chat interface with conversation memory. Switch between Gemini 2.5 Pro, Claude 4, GPT-4o, and Groq models. Ask questions mid-session without leaving the app.

Quick Ask Widget

A floating button always visible in the corner — no matter what page you're on. Get a quick answer without interrupting your timer.

AI Writer

A distraction-free writing editor with 9 AI actions: Improve, Shorten, Expand, Fix Grammar, Simplify, Summarise, Change Tone, Translate (9 languages), and Cite Source.

AI Tools

Standalone utilities: Assignment Checker (grade prediction + gap analysis), AI Detector, and Humanizer — paste any text and get results instantly.

Focus Reminders

When Focus Guard catches you off-task, a nudge appears immediately. An AI-generated message follows — personalised to your actual study goals, not a generic reminder.

Lecture Transcription

Record mic audio during class and transcribe it via OpenAI Whisper or Groq. The transcript drops straight into your session notes.

Citation Generator

Describe a source, pick APA, MLA, or Chicago, and the AI formats a ready-to-paste citation. Inserts at cursor position in the AI Writer.

How It Works

Six short tests. One honest picture of how you learn.

You'll complete six timed activities — each one designed to measure a different part of how your brain handles information. None of them require any prior knowledge. When you finish, the AI reads all your results together and writes you a personal report explaining your strengths, where you can improve, and exactly how to study in a way that works for you.

Memory Span

A sequence of numbers appears one at a time. Remember them in order and type them back. Each round gets a little longer — this shows how much information you can hold in mind at once.

Memory

Symbol Matching

A small chart shows which symbol matches which number. A symbol flashes — tap the right number as quickly as you can. This measures how fast your brain can scan and match information.

Speed

Focus Test

Letters flash on screen one by one. Press the spacebar only when you see the letter X — and only then. It sounds simple, but keeping your concentration steady the whole time is the real challenge.

Attention

Pattern Puzzles

A grid of pictures has one piece missing. Four options are shown — pick the one that completes the pattern. Each puzzle gets a little harder. This measures how well you spot patterns and think logically.

Reasoning

Reading & Understanding

Read three short passages at your own pace, then answer questions about each one. This shows how quickly and accurately you can pull meaning from written text.

Reading

Rule Switching

Shapes appear and you sort them by a rule — but the rule changes without warning. This tests how quickly and cleanly your brain can switch gears from one way of thinking to another.

Adaptability

Your Cognitive Report

When all six tests are done, the AI reads your results together and writes a full report: where you stand in each area, what your natural strengths are, and a 90-day plan built to develop the areas where you have the most room to grow.
