Features

Everything in one place

No tabs, no subscriptions, no distractions. Just the tools you actually need to study well.

4 Focus Timers

Pomodoro 25, Focus 45, Deep Work 60, and Marathon 90. Each has an animated ring and auto-awards Study Points scaled to how long you study.

Focus Mode

Focus Guard

Define which apps are allowed during a session. Switch to anything else and the app automatically snaps you back. AI generates personalised nudges when you go off-task.

Focus Mode

Calendar

Month, week, and list views. Add study sessions and meetings with meeting links (Zoom, Meet, Teams). Upcoming events trigger native 5-minute notifications. Emails auto-add detected meetings.

Calendar

Session Records

Log study sessions manually with date, subject, duration, and a 1–5 star rating. Screen recording and lecture audio transcription built in.

Records

Meeting Notes

A rich-text notes editor linked to your sessions. Save, load, and export notes. Transcribe lecture audio directly to notes with one click.

Records

Email Inbox

Connect Gmail, iCloud, or any IMAP account. Meeting invites are auto-detected and added to your Calendar with one click.

Inbox
Artificial Intelligence

AI that actually helps you study

Bring your own API key (Gemini, Claude, GPT-4, or Groq). Every AI feature in the app uses the same key — set it once.

AI Tutor

A full chat interface with system-prompt control. Switch between Gemini 2.5 Pro, Claude 4, GPT-4o, and more. Conversation memory included.

Quick Ask Widget

A floating chat bubble always visible in the bottom-right corner — no matter what page you're on. Ask a quick question mid-session without switching tabs.

Study Kit Generator

Paste your notes and the AI generates a summary, flip flashcards, and exam Q&A pairs in one go. Export flashcards as CSV for Anki. Or switch to Personalised Study Guide mode — powered by your cognitive assessment — for a phase-plan, schedule, and techniques built for how you actually think.

AI Writer

A distraction-free writing editor with 9 AI actions: Improve, Shorten, Expand, Fix Grammar, Simplify, Summarise, Change Tone, Translate (9 languages), and Cite Source.

AI Tools

Standalone tools: Assignment Checker (grade prediction + gap analysis), AI Detector, and Humanizer — paste any text and get instant results.

Smart Focus Reminders

When Focus Guard catches you off-task, an instant nudge appears. Then an AI-generated personalised motivational message follows — tailored to your actual study goals.

Lecture Audio Transcription

Record mic audio during lectures and transcribe it via OpenAI Whisper or Groq. The transcript drops straight into your Meeting Notes editor.

Citation Generator

Describe a source, pick APA / MLA / Chicago, and the AI formats a ready-to-paste citation. Inserts at cursor in the AI Writer.

Supported AI providers: Google Gemini (Gemini 2.5 Pro / Flash), Anthropic Claude (Claude 4 Sonnet/Opus), OpenAI (GPT-4o / GPT-4o-mini), Groq (Llama 3, Whisper). Free tiers available on all providers.
New in v4.0

Know your brain.
Study your way.

Most study apps treat every student the same. Ur Study Bro v4.0 starts differently — with a real psychoeducational cognitive battery, the same type of assessment that costs thousands of dollars at a specialist clinic.

🧠
Psychoeducational Cognitive Battery

6 real cognitive tests. 30 items each. AI analysis.

Each test is an actual timed cognitive task — not a questionnaire. When you finish, the AI analyses your raw scores, estimates percentiles in each domain, identifies your cognitive strengths and weaknesses, and writes a fully personalised study plan.

Digit Span

Sequences of digits are shown one at a time. Recall them in order. Span increases from 3 to 12 across 30 trials — tests working memory capacity and verbal rehearsal.

Working Memory

Symbol Search

A key of symbol-to-number pairs stays on screen. A symbol flashes — tap the matching number as fast as you can. 30 items measure processing speed and visual scanning.

Processing Speed

Continuous Performance Test

Letters flash rapidly. Press Space only when you see X. 30 target appearances, mixed with distractors. Scores hit rate, false alarms, and d-prime sensitivity — clinically validated attention measure.

Sustained Attention

Matrix Reasoning

A 3×3 grid of emoji patterns with one cell missing. Choose the correct piece from 4 options. 30 progressive puzzles test fluid intelligence and non-verbal pattern recognition.

Fluid Reasoning

Reading Rate & Comprehension

Read 3 passages at your natural pace and click Done. Then answer 10 comprehension questions per passage — 30 total. Scores WPM and comprehension accuracy across all passages.

Reading

Task Switching

Coloured shapes appear. The rule alternates between "sort by colour" and "sort by shape" without warning. 30 trials measure cognitive flexibility and switch cost — how much mental effort a context change costs you.
