Features

Everything you need to study well.

No subscriptions. No account. No clutter. Just the tools that actually matter.

Focus Timers

Pomodoro 25, Focus 45, Deep Work 60, Marathon 90. Animated ring, automatic breaks, custom timers, and Study Points on completion.

Focus Guard

Choose which apps are allowed during a session. Open anything else and the app hides it and brings you back — automatically.

Calendar

Month, week, and list views. Add sessions and meetings with video links. 5-minute native notifications. Meeting invites auto-import from email.

Session Records

Log sessions with subject, rating, and notes. Built-in screen recorder and lecture audio transcription. Everything stored locally.

Email Inbox

Connect Gmail, iCloud, or any IMAP account. Class and meeting invites are detected and added to your Calendar in one click.

Study Analytics

Weekly hours, daily streaks, subject breakdown, and peak focus times — calculated automatically from your real session history.

Study Kit

Paste notes and get a summary, flashcards, and practice questions instantly. Export to Anki. Or generate a full personalised study guide from your cognitive profile.

100% Offline & Private

No cloud, no telemetry, no sign-up. Everything lives on your device. Works on a plane, in a library, anywhere — no internet needed.

Artificial Intelligence

AI tools, built into your workflow.

Bring your own API key — Gemini, Claude, GPT-4, or Groq. Set it once. Every feature uses it.

AI Tutor

Full chat with memory. Switch between Gemini 2.5 Pro, Claude 4, GPT-4o, and Groq. Ask questions without leaving your timer.

Quick Ask Widget

A floating button on every page — get a quick answer mid-session without switching tabs or breaking focus.

Personalised Study Guide

After your cognitive assessment, generate a full subject guide — phases, daily schedule, techniques matched to your learning style, and weekly targets.

AI Writer

Distraction-free editor with 9 AI actions: Improve, Shorten, Expand, Fix Grammar, Simplify, Summarise, Change Tone, Translate, and Cite Source.

AI Tools

Assignment Checker (grade prediction + gap analysis), AI Detector, and Humanizer — paste any text and get results instantly.

Lecture Transcription

Record mic audio during class and transcribe it via Whisper or Groq. The transcript drops directly into your session notes.

Providers: Google Gemini · Anthropic Claude · OpenAI GPT-4o · Groq — free tiers available on all four.

Cognitive Assessment

A learning profile built around you.

Most study apps give every student the same advice. Ur Study Bro starts by understanding how your mind actually works.

Six short tests. One honest picture of how you learn.

You'll complete six timed activities — each designed to measure a different part of how your brain handles information. No prior knowledge needed. When you finish, the AI reads all your results together and writes you a personal report explaining your strengths, your growth areas, and exactly how to study in a way that works for you.

Memory Span

A sequence of numbers appears one at a time. Remember them in order and type them back. Each round gets slightly longer.

Symbol Matching

A chart shows which symbol matches which number. A symbol flashes — tap the right number as quickly as you can.

Focus Test

Letters flash on screen one by one. Press the spacebar only when you see the letter X — keeping concentration steady the whole time is the challenge.

Pattern Puzzles

A grid of images has one piece missing. Four options are shown — pick the one that completes the pattern. Gets harder with each round.

Reading & Understanding

Read three short passages at your own pace, then answer questions about each one. Tests how quickly and accurately you pull meaning from text.

Rule Switching

Shapes appear and you sort them by a rule — but the rule changes without warning. Tests how quickly your brain can switch from one way of thinking to another.

FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before you download.

Yes — completely free, forever. No subscription, no free trial, no premium tier. The app is MIT-licensed and open source. You can download it, modify it, and use it however you like.
No account required — ever. The app works entirely offline. Your data stays on your device. An internet connection is only needed when you actively use the AI features (which require an API key you control).
Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT-4o, and Groq. All four have free tiers. You bring your own API key — Ur Study Bro never sees or stores it beyond your device. Set it once in Settings and every AI feature uses it automatically.
macOS (Apple Silicon + Intel), Windows (x64), and Linux (AppImage). A mobile version for iOS and Android is planned. The app is built with Electron so it behaves identically across platforms.
The six tests are adapted from well-established clinical instruments used in psychoeducational assessments: Digit Span (WISC/WAIS), AX-CPT, Symbol Search, Matrix Reasoning, Reading Comprehension, and Task Switching. The app is an educational tool, not a medical device — results are not a formal diagnosis. For a clinical assessment, consult a licensed psychologist.
All four providers have free tiers with no credit card required to start:
  • Google Gemini → aistudio.google.com/apikey
  • Anthropic Claude → console.anthropic.com
  • OpenAI GPT-4o → platform.openai.com/api-keys
  • Groq → console.groq.com/keys
Once you have a key, paste it into Settings → AI Settings inside the app.
Yes! The project is open source under the MIT License. You can open an issue for bug reports or feature requests, or submit a pull request. All contributions are welcome.
When you start a timer with Focus Guard on, you choose which apps are allowed (e.g. your notes app, a browser). Every 2 seconds, the system checks which app is in the foreground. If it's not on your allowed list, the app hides it at the system level and brings Ur Study Bro back to the front. On macOS, this uses AppleScript to set the app's visibility. You can only disable Focus Guard by resetting the timer — and sessions ended this way don't earn points.
