Artificial Intelligence

AI that actually helps you study.

Runs on Ollama by default — fully local, no API key, no internet required. Cloud providers (Gemini, Claude, GPT-4o, Groq) available if you want them.

AI Tutor

Full chat with memory. Switch between Gemini 2.5 Pro, Claude 4, GPT-4o, and Groq. Ask questions without leaving your timer.

Quick Ask Widget

A floating button on every page — get a quick answer mid-session without switching tabs or breaking focus.

Personalised Study Guide

After your cognitive assessment, generate a full subject guide — phases, daily schedule, techniques matched to your learning style, and weekly targets.

AI Writer

Distraction-free editor with 9 AI actions: Improve, Shorten, Expand, Fix Grammar, Simplify, Summarise, Change Tone, Translate, and Cite Source.

AI Tools

Assignment Checker (grade prediction + gap analysis), AI Detector, and Humanizer — paste any text and get results instantly.

Lecture Transcription

Record mic audio during class and transcribe it via Whisper or Groq. The transcript drops directly into your session notes.

Local (default): Ollama — runs 100% on your machine, no API key needed  ·  Cloud: Google Gemini · Anthropic Claude · OpenAI GPT-4o · Groq — free tiers available on all four.
