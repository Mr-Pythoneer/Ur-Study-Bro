One-click recording

Select your screen or any open window as the source. Press record — that's it. No setup, no extra software. The video is saved locally the moment you stop.

Stored on your device

Every recording stays on your machine — never uploaded, never shared. Browse, play back, or delete from inside the app at any time.

Audio transcription

Record your mic during a lecture and transcribe it using Whisper or Groq. The full transcript drops straight into your session notes — searchable, copyable, shareable.

Linked to your notes

Recordings appear right inside Meeting Records alongside your written notes, ratings, and session metadata — everything for a class in one place.

Any window, any app

Capture your entire display or just one specific window — your slides, browser, Zoom call, whatever you're studying from. Switch sources between sessions.

Private by default

Screen Recording permission is requested once on first launch. The app never runs the recorder in the background and never transmits any video data off your device.
