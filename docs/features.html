Features

Everything you actually need. Nothing you don't.

No subscription. No sign-up. No nonsense. I just wanted a study app that worked, so I built one.

Focus Timers

Pomodoro 25, Focus 45, Deep Work 60, Marathon 90. Animated ring, automatic breaks, custom timers, and Study Points on completion.

Focus Guard

Choose which apps are allowed during a session. Open anything else and the app hides it and brings you back — automatically.

Calendar

Month, week, and list views. Add sessions and meetings with video links. 5-minute native notifications. Meeting invites auto-import from email.

Session Records

Log sessions with subject, rating, and notes. Built-in screen recorder and lecture audio transcription. Everything stored locally.

Email Inbox

Connect Gmail, iCloud, or any IMAP account. Class and meeting invites are detected and added to your Calendar in one click.

Study Analytics

Weekly hours, daily streaks, subject breakdown, and peak focus times — calculated automatically from your real session history.

Study Kit

Paste notes and get a summary, flashcards, and practice questions instantly. Export to Anki. Or generate a full personalised study guide from your cognitive profile.

100% Offline & Private

No cloud, no telemetry, no sign-up. Everything lives on your device. Works on a plane, in a library, anywhere — no internet needed.

Cognitive Assessment

A learning profile that's actually about you.

Most apps give you the same generic tips. This one runs six mini-tests first and builds your study plan from the results.

Six mini-tests → one honest look at how you study.

Based on the same methods used in real psychology research, but designed to be quick and (genuinely) kind of fun. Takes about 15 minutes. You get a full report at the end — what your attention looks like, how your memory works, how you learn best, and a personalised study blueprint you can actually use.

Tap the Target — Focus & Impulse Control

A reaction game: tap when you see the green circle, hold back when you see red. 120 rounds, about 3 minutes. Measures how well you can focus and stop yourself from making mistakes.

Attention Quiz

6 quick questions about how you normally focus — things like "how often do you lose track of what you're doing?" Totally anonymous, no right answers.

Number Memory

Numbers flash on screen one at a time — type them back in order. Gets longer as you go. Then you do it in reverse order (heard aloud). Surprisingly tricky and kind of addictive.

How You Learn

3 everyday scenarios — pick whichever answer feels most like you. Figures out whether you learn best by seeing, listening, reading, or doing. Your study path is built around your result.

Brain Switcher

Sort coloured shapes by a rule that changes every 5 rounds — without warning. Tests how fast your brain can switch gears. Most people find this one harder than it looks.

Stress Check

Three sliders — brain effort, time pressure, frustration. Takes 30 seconds. The app uses your answers to figure out ideal session length (higher stress = shorter, more frequent sessions).

⚡
Attention & Executive Function Narrative
A detailed paragraph interpreting your CPT results — reaction time, false alarms, and missed targets — in the context of real-world focus and impulse control.
💾
Working Memory Profile
Forward and backward span scores with an explanation of the gap between them and what it means for how you should structure your revision.
🎨
Learning Style Analysis
Your VARK breakdown with specific study techniques recommended for your dominant modality — not generic advice, but methods matched to how you actually process information.
🔀
Cognitive Flexibility Score
Your switching cost in milliseconds — how much your brain slows down when a rule changes — plus whether that means you should use single-subject or mixed-subject sessions.
📋
Optimal Study Blueprint
Five personalised cards: ideal session length, environment setup, task sequencing strategy, review method, and one specific warning sign to watch for during study.
🗺️
"Make My Study Plan" button
One click turns your full cognitive profile into a 40-task Duolingo-style path, personalised to your scores. XP, streaks, and daily goals keep you on track.

Infinite & subject-specific

Add your actual subjects (e.g. "AP Calculus", "History Essay"). Every task references your real material — not generic study tips. New batches generate automatically as you complete tasks.

Adapts to your weaknesses

The app tracks which task types you complete most and least. Each new batch skews toward your gaps — more memory drills if you avoid them, harder challenges if you breeze through easy tasks.

Growth tab + auto Focus Mode

See your progress: tasks done, XP earned, days active, type breakdown, milestones. Session tasks automatically open Focus Mode — complete the task and your timer starts instantly.
